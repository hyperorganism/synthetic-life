# Origins and Synthetic Life

How does living organization arise, and what can be constructed? Historical origins, bottom-up chemistry, genome minimization and reorganization of existing living tissue are distinct experimental programs.

## Prebiotic chemistry

- **Abiotic amino-acid synthesis** - A week of electric discharge through circulating methane, ammonia, hydrogen and water produces glycine and alanines. Paper chromatography identifies the products; the experiment tests synthesis under a supplied reducing gas mixture. [Miller 1953](<https://doi.org/10.1126/science.117.3046.528> "A Production of Amino Acids Under Possible Primitive Earth Conditions; Science").
- **Cyanosulfidic protometabolism** - Laboratory reactions produce RNA, amino-acid and lipid precursors through shared hydrogen-cyanide chemistry. Key steps use UV light and hydrogen sulfide. Ordered reagent additions are required. [Patel et al. 2015](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4568310/> "Common origins of RNA, protein and lipid precursors in a cyanosulfidic protometabolism; Nature Chemistry").

## Replication and metabolism hypotheses

- **Hypercycles** - Couples replicators in a catalytic cycle: each promotes replication of the next. Proposed to integrate information across shorter replicating units under copying-error constraints; the construction is a theoretical organization of replication. [Eigen and Schuster 1977](<https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00450633> "A principle of natural self-organization; Naturwissenschaften").
- **RNA world** - Proposes an early stage in which RNA supplies both inherited information and catalysis, before DNA genomes and protein enzymes. Catalytic RNA motivates the scenario; the historical pathway requires additional chemical evidence. [Gilbert 1986](<https://doi.org/10.1038/319618a0> "Origin of life: The RNA world; Nature").
- **Surface metabolism** - Proposes autocatalytic chemistry on mineral surfaces before enzymes or templates. Pyrite formation supplies both a binding surface and energy for carbon fixation; the proposed sequence leads from surface networks toward cells and genetics. [Wächtershäuser 1988](<https://doi.org/10.1128/mr.52.4.452-484.1988> "Before enzymes and templates: theory of surface metabolism; Microbiol. Rev.").

## Autonomy and viability

- **Autopoiesis** - Defines a system through a network of processes that produces its components and maintains the unity they constitute. Varela, Maturana and Uribe illustrate this account of organization with a computational model. [Varela et al. 1974](<https://doi.org/10.1016/0303-2647(74)90031-8> "Autopoiesis: The organization of living systems, its characterization and a model; Biosystems").
- **Kinetic proofreading** - Fuel-driven reactions let enzymes reject incorrect substrates at successive steps. Hopfield's kinetic model reduces biosynthetic errors beyond equilibrium discrimination, connecting energy consumption to molecular accuracy. [Hopfield 1974](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC434344/> "Kinetic Proofreading: A New Mechanism for Reducing Errors in Biosynthetic Processes Requiring High Specificity; PNAS").
- **Autocatalytic sets** - A theoretical reaction network lets molecules catalyze one another's formation. Increasing molecular diversity makes collectively catalytic subsets likely under the model's random-catalysis assumptions. [Kauffman 1986](<https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-5193(86)80047-9> "Autocatalytic sets of proteins; J. Theor. Biol.").
- **Closure of constraints** - Models biological organization as constraints that channel processes which maintain other constraints in the system. Organizational closure permits exchange of matter and energy with the environment. [Montévil and Mossio 2015](<https://montevil.org/publications/articles/2015-mm-organisation-closure-constraints/> "Biological organisation as closure of constraints; J. Theor. Biol.").
- **Information and viability** - Intervenes on system-environment correlations to identify information contributing to a chosen viability measure. The result depends on that measure, system boundary and timescale; [low entropy need not imply continued life](https://hyperorganism.github.io/recognizing-life/#definitions-and-organizational-criteria). [Kolchinsky and Wolpert 2018](<https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08053v3> "Semantic information, autonomous agency and non-equilibrium statistical physics; Interface Focus").
- **Antithetic integral feedback** - In engineered E. coli, binding regulators implement integral feedback. Feedback circuits restore mean protein levels after induced degradation and maintain growth after cooling from 37 to 30 C, unlike open-loop controls. [Aoki et al. 2019](<https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1321-1> "A universal biomolecular integral feedback controller for robust perfect adaptation; Nature").

## Compartments and heredity

- **Cross-replicating RNA** - Two RNA enzymes catalyze each other's assembly from four supplied oligonucleotide substrates. Serial transfer sustains amplification; competing variants generate recombinant replicators. [Lincoln and Joyce 2009](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2652413/> "Self-Sustained Replication of an RNA Enzyme; Science").
- **Protocell membranes** - Feeding fatty-acid vesicles produces elongated compartments that divide under gentle shear. Encapsulated RNA passes to daughter vesicles; the experiment couples membrane growth and division without replicating the RNA. [Zhu and Szostak 2009](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2669828/> "Coupled Growth and Division of Model Protocell Membranes; JACS").
- **XNA heredity** - Engineered polymerases copy DNA into six synthetic nucleic-acid backbones and back. Selected hexitol nucleic acid sequences bind RNA or protein targets. DNA intermediates connect successive generations. [Pinheiro et al. 2012](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3362463/> "Synthetic genetic polymers capable of heredity and evolution; Science").
- **Hachimoji** - Eight nucleotide letters form four pairing types with predictable DNA duplex stability. An engineered polymerase transcribes the expanded alphabet into a designed RNA that binds a dye and makes it fluoresce. [Hoshika et al. 2019](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6413494/> "Hachimoji DNA and RNA: A genetic system with eight building blocks; Science").
- **QT45** - A 45-nucleotide RNA catalyst synthesizes itself and its complementary strand from supplied short RNA substrates in separate reactions in ice. Each yields about 0.2% product after 72 days. A sustained replication cycle has not been demonstrated. [Gianni et al. 2026](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7618777/> "A small polymerase ribozyme that can synthesize itself and its complementary strand; Science").
- **DNA replication and lipid synthesis** - A six-gene program drives both processes in vesicles with supplied protein-making machinery. Changing template preparation raises the fraction with both activities from about 8% to 30%. Lipid yields remain too low for membrane growth. [Restrepo Sierra et al. 2026](<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-69531-9> "A synthetic cell with integrated DNA self-replication and lipid biosynthesis; Nature Communications").

## Minimal cells and engineered living constructs

- **JCVI-syn1.0** - A chemically synthesized 1.08-Mb genome takes over a living recipient cell and supports continued replication. Assembly uses yeast, and the recipient supplies the initial cytoplasm and machinery. The design largely copies an existing bacterial genome. [Gibson et al. 2010](<https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1190719> "Creation of a Bacterial Cell Controlled by a Chemically Synthesized Genome; Science").
- **Minimal genome (JCVI-syn3.0)** - A 473-gene cell grows in rich medium. Deleting individually dispensable genes together can kill the cell. Genes needed for robust growth are retained. In 2016, 149 genes still lacked a specific functional assignment. [Hutchison et al. 2016](<https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aad6253> "Design and synthesis of a minimal bacterial genome; Science").
    - **Division in minimal cells** - JCVI-syn3.0 grows with irregular shapes. Restoring the FtsZ-containing gene cluster alone does not correct them. A seven-gene combination restores near-normal morphology, separating genes needed for growth from those needed for regular division. [Pelletier et al. 2021](<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2021.03.008> "Genetic requirements for cell division in a genomically minimal cell; Cell").
    - **Evolution of a minimal cell** - The 493-gene JCVI-syn3B initially has reduced fitness. Over about 2,000 generations of laboratory serial passage, evolved cells recover effectively all of the measured deficit. This tests adaptation under the supplied growth regime. [Moger-Reischer et al. 2023](<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06288-x> "Evolution of a minimal cell; Nature").
- **Xenobots** - Evolutionary search designs cell arrangements for locomotion; selected designs are manually built from frog embryonic skin and heart cells. Physical trials test transfer of simulated morphology and behavior into living tissue. [Kriegman et al. 2020](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6994979/> "A scalable pipeline for designing reconfigurable organisms; PNAS"); [code](<https://github.com/skriegman/reconfigurable_organisms>).
- **Anthrobots** - Cultured adult human airway cells self-organize into cilia-driven motile constructs. Fused assemblies placed across scratches in neuronal monolayers promoted local closure where they maintained contact with both sides. [Gumuskaya et al. 2024](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10811512/> "Motile Living Biobots Self-Construct from Adult Human Somatic Progenitor Seed Cells; Advanced Science").

## Open questions

- Can information-bearing replication and energy-driven reaction networks be coupled under compatible chemistry, without externally scheduling each step?
- Which constructions couple component production, boundary maintenance and heritable reproduction, and which stages still require scheduled intervention?
- Which viability measures distinguish continued organization from persistent shape or low entropy alone?
